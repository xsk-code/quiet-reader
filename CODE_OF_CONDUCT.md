# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be civil and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before rewriting someone else's work.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's articles, credentials, logs or
  account details.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic and free of harassment, discrimination,
  threats or spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participation for behavior that violates this code. Reports should go to the
maintainer through a private channel; do not include sensitive third-party data.
Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and kept confidential where
possible.
